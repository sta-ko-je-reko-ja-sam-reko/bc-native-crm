# FEAT-OPP-001 - Opportunity Depth

Turn a basic opportunity into a full deal record: the products you expect to sell, the competitors you are up
against, the people involved, and an owner and rating — with the estimated value adding up for you.

## Add the products you expect to sell

1. Open an **Opportunity** card.
2. In the **Lines**, add a row for each item or resource you expect to sell, with a **Quantity** and **Unit
   Price**. The **Line Amount** is calculated for you.
3. The **CRM Estimated Revenue** on the card adds up all the lines automatically.

## Record competitors and stakeholders

1. On the opportunity, open **Competitors** and add each competitor with a **Threat Level** and their **Strengths**
   and **Weaknesses**.
2. Open **Stakeholders** and add the contacts involved, each with a **Role** (for example decision maker,
   influencer, champion, blocker or end user).

## Set the owner and rating

1. In the **CRM** group, set the **CRM Owner** (a salesperson or team) and a **CRM Rating** (hot, warm or cold).

## Close the deal

1. Set the opportunity's status to **Won** or **Lost** as usual.
2. A closing activity is logged automatically against the opportunity, so the outcome shows on the timeline.
