# FEAT-ACT-001 - Activities and Timeline

Keep a single, chronological record of every interaction — tasks, phone calls, appointments, emails and notes —
against any customer or contact, and see it all on one timeline.

## Log an activity

1. Open a **Customer** or **Contact** card.
2. In the **CRM** group, choose **New activity**.
3. Pick an **Activity Type** (task, phone call, appointment, email or note) and enter a **Subject**.
4. Optionally set the **Priority**, **Direction** (incoming or outgoing), an **Activity Date** and a **Due Date**,
   and add the details in **Description**.
5. Choose an **Owner** so it is clear who is responsible.
6. Close the card. The activity is saved and appears on the timeline.

## Complete an activity

1. Open the activity from the timeline or the **CRM Activities** list.
2. Set **Status** to *Completed* (or *Canceled*). The completion time is stamped automatically.

## Read the timeline

1. On the **Customer** or **Contact** card, find the **CRM Timeline** on the right-hand side.
2. The most recent activity appears at the top; scroll to see the full history.
3. Choose an entry on the timeline to open that activity for the full detail.

The timeline always follows the record you are looking at — open a different customer and it shows that
customer's activities.
