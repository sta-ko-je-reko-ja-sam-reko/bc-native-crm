# FEAT-OWN-001 - Ownership and Teams

Give every customer, contact and opportunity a clear owner — a salesperson or a whole team — so everyone knows who
is responsible, and each seller can focus on just their own records.

## Set up a team

1. Choose the search icon and enter **CRM Teams**, then open it.
2. Choose **New** and fill in a **Code** and **Name** for the team (for example a region or a product squad).
3. Optionally pick a **Team Lead Salesperson**.
4. Open the team's **Members** and add each salesperson who belongs to it. Mark one member as **Team Lead** if you
   like. The **Member Count** updates automatically.

## Give a record an owner

1. Open a **Customer**, **Contact** or **Opportunity** card.
2. In the **CRM** group, set the **CRM Owner Type** to *Salesperson* or *Team*.
3. In **CRM Owner**, pick the salesperson or team that owns the record.

New customers and contacts are given a default owner automatically when they are created, so nothing is left
unassigned.

## See only your records

1. Open the **Customers** or **Contacts** list.
2. Choose the **Show my CRM records** action to filter the list down to the records you own.
3. Run the action again to return to the full list.

> Note: ownership is used for focus and reporting. To *hard-restrict* which records a user can see, ask your
> administrator to apply a security filter on the CRM permission set.
