# FEAT-BPF-001 - Business Process Flow

Guide your team through a deal one stage at a time, with a visual progress bar across the top of the opportunity so
everyone can see where things stand.

## Follow the process on an opportunity

1. Open an **Opportunity** card. A **process bar** shows the stages of your sales process across the top, with the
   current stage highlighted.
2. Work the deal as normal. When a stage is complete, choose the next stage on the bar (or the **Advance** action)
   to move forward.
3. The bar records when the process was started and, once you reach the end, when it was completed.

## Review or adjust the stages

1. Choose the search icon, enter **CRM Processes**, and open it.
2. Open a process to see its ordered **Stages**. A default "Lead to Opportunity" process is provided out of the box.
3. Mark one process as **Active** for its record type so new opportunities pick it up.

> Tip: the process bar is about *where you are* in the journey; it works alongside the opportunity's own status and
> the estimated value.
