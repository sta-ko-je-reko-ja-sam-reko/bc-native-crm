# FEAT-RC-001 - Role Center

A home page built for salespeople — your open work at a glance, and one-click access to everything in the CRM.

## Make it your home page

1. Choose **Settings** (the gear icon) and then **My Settings**.
2. Set the **Role** to **CRM Manager** and choose **OK**, then reload when prompted.
3. You now open onto the CRM home page each time you sign in.

## Use the home page

- The tiles show your work: **My Open Activities**, **Overdue Activities**, **My Opportunities** and
  **Opportunities In Progress**. Choose a tile to open the records behind it.
- Use the **navigation** down the side to jump to Teams, Activities, Opportunities and Processes.
- Choose **New activity** to log an interaction without leaving the home page.
- The **Governance** actions let an administrator turn on audit logging and find duplicate customers.

The tiles are personal — they always count *your* activities and opportunities, based on the salesperson you are
linked to.
