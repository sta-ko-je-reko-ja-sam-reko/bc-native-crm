# FEAT-PTY-001 - Party Enrichment

Capture the extra business detail a CRM needs on your customers and contacts — who owns whom, their industry and
size, and how they have agreed to be contacted.

## Record classification and firmographics

1. Open a **Customer** card.
2. In the **CRM Classification** group, set:
   - **CRM Parent Customer** — the parent company, if this customer is part of a group.
   - **CRM Industry Group**, **CRM Annual Revenue** and **CRM No. of Employees**.

## Record contact preferences and consent

1. On the **Customer** or **Contact** card, open the **CRM Preferences** group.
2. Set the **CRM Preferred Contact Method**.
3. Switch on **CRM Do Not Email**, **CRM Do Not Phone** or **CRM Do Not Bulk Email** to respect the party's
   wishes. Keep these current — your team should check them before reaching out.

## See a company's subsidiaries

1. On a parent customer's card, choose the **Subsidiaries** action.
2. The list opens filtered to the customers whose parent is this company.
