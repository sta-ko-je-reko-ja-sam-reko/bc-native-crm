# FEAT-LNK-001 - Transaction Pipeline Linkage

Trace a deal all the way through: link an order back to the opportunity it came from, give it a sales status that
your team can follow, and see — right from the opportunity — the orders and invoices it turned into.

## Link an order to its opportunity

1. Open a **Sales Quote** or **Sales Order**.
2. In the **CRM** group, set the **CRM Opportunity No.** to the opportunity the deal came from.
3. The order now traces back to the pipeline — and any invoice you post from it will carry the same link.

## Follow the sales status

1. On the order, the **CRM Sales Status** starts as **Active**.
2. Use **Submit (CRM)**, **Mark fulfilled (CRM)** or **Cancel (CRM)** to move the status as the deal progresses.
   This is a sales status only — it does not change how the order is posted.
3. Use **Lock pricing (CRM)** to record that the prices on the order have been frozen.

## Post as usual

1. Ship and invoice the order the normal way. Nothing about posting changes.
2. The **posted invoice** automatically shows the **CRM Opportunity No.** it came from, so billing stays connected
   to the deal.

## See the results on the opportunity

1. Open the **Opportunity** card.
2. In the **CRM Transactions** group you can see how many **Linked Orders** and **Linked Invoices** came from this
   opportunity.
3. Choose **Linked orders** or **Linked invoices** to open the full list and drill into any one of them.

> Before you can use this, an administrator turns the feature on in **Pipeline Linkage Setup** and the session
> restarts.
