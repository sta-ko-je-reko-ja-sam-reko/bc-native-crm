# FEAT-GOV-001 - Governance

Keep your CRM data trustworthy: track who changed what, and find duplicate customers and contacts before they
cause confusion.

## Turn on audit logging

1. From the CRM home page, open the **Governance** actions and choose **Enable CRM audit logging** (an
   administrator does this once).
2. From then on, changes to the CRM records — teams, activities, opportunity lines and process state — are tracked.
3. To review the history, choose the search icon, enter **Change Log Entries**, and open it.

## Find duplicate customers

1. From the **Governance** actions, choose **Find duplicate customers**.
2. If any customers share the same name, the list opens showing only those, so you can review and merge or correct
   them. If there are none, you are told so.

## Find duplicate contacts

1. Use the same **Find duplicates** approach from the contacts area to surface contacts that share a name.

> Note: Business Central already warns about duplicate contacts as you create them; this adds the same safety net
> for customers and lets you review existing duplicates in bulk.
